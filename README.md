# Space Riders Kart

A free retro kart racer. Train with Drill Sergeant on the Moon, then race 8 Space Riders through Saturn's Rings, the Andromeda Casino Strip, the Space Station Loop and the Void Rift. Drift for mini-turbos and grab items (missiles, forcefields, wave disrupters, tractor beams, lasers and lightning). Finish top 3 to clear each level and unlock Draco, Eyereenia, Silas and Drill Sergeant's tank. Pick your craft (Moon Rover, hover-saucer or mini spaceship), win free-play races to unlock the Hover Board, Comet Racer, Ring Glider, Star Cruiser and SR Starfire, and clear every level for a numbered Certified Victor poster.

Plays on PC (keyboard or gamepad) and phones (on-screen controls).

Play: https://sanyziahj.github.io/space-riders-kart/
